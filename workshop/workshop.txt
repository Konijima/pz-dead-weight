description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=Walk onto the beam scale's plate. The beam head reads your weight (about 2.5 seconds to settle, like the real thing). Walk off and the number goes with you. No item, no menu, nothing to craft.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]A brass and cream beam head readout, poise sliding toward your weight and settling instead of snapping, because a real scale wobbles.
description=[*]One decimal of precision.
description=[*]Left click the readout to switch between kg and lb. Your choice is remembered between sessions.
description=[*]Each scale has its own look: the clinic scale its beam head, the Digital Scale a plain native panel.
description=[*]New in 1.3.0, Build 42 only: the Digital Scale, a small home scale you place on the floor or on a counter (see below).
description=[*]The character screen's Info tab now shows only your weight category in words (Emaciated to Obese) with its trend arrow. The exact number stays on the scale.
description=[*]Your survivor turns once to face the scale's column on stopping, because reading a number sideways is undignified.
description=[*]Two short sounds: stepping on, stepping off.
description=[*]A "Step on Scale" option at the top of the scale's right click menu, with its own small hand icon, walks you there. Holding an animal (Build 42), a "Put Animal on Scale" option sets it down on the plate from the next square, so it is weighed without you.
description=[*]Every local splitscreen player gets their own readout.
description=[*]The scale reads everyone on it, survivors, animals and zombies, added up. Stand a couple of squares away (a sandbox option, 2 by default) and you read them too, like a doctor weighing a patient.
description=[*]No per frame scanning: the reading only exists while someone is on the scale and you are next to it.
description=[*]28 languages, every one the game ships.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]The Digital Scale (Build 42 only)[/h2]
description=A small bathroom scale, furniture you place, turn (four facings) and pick up again, on the floor or on a counter. It reads 0 to 130 kg on a plain native panel; the clinic scale keeps its beam head. On the floor it weighs people, animals and zombies standing on it, and items lying on it. On a counter it weighs the items you set on it, which are lifted onto its plate; a survivor just walking through a counter scale's square is not weighed, but climbing through a window over it reads. Stepping onto a Digital Scale turns you to look at its screen, and you only read a scale you are facing: turn away and the readout goes, turn back and it returns.
description=
description=It also turns up in the world, only in new loot and in areas of the map nobody has explored yet (a room you already visited never gets one), only in homes and motels, never in a public restroom, and never in front of a toilet, basin or other fixture:
description=[list]
description=[*]inside bathroom counters, the cupboards under a basin (a counter never holds two, and a bathroom gives out at most one from its counters);
description=[*]on the floor against a wall in a big enough bathroom.
description=[/list]
description=Build 41 has no Digital Scale, only the clinic scale.
description=
description=[h2]Weigh others, and anything else[/h2]
description=Whatever stands on the scale is weighed, and anyone on it or within reach in the same room and in plain sight (2 squares by default, a sandbox option) reads the same number. Two survivors together read their true combined weight: the beam rests at its end and the number stays honest. A light animal reads under 35 kg, a heavy pair over 130 kg.

description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Konijima/pz-dead-weight/main/workshop/art/deadweight-animal.gif[/img]

description=[h2]Options[/h2]
description=Left click the readout for kg or lb. Your choice is remembered automatically.
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=Page "Dead Weight" in the sandbox options, all off or at their default until you change them:
description=[list]
description=[*]Weigh what you carry: adds worn items and bag contents to each survivor's reading. In multiplayer everyone in reach reads the same total.
description=[*]Weigh the whole square: count anything in the scale's square, not only what is on the plate.
description=[*]Reading distance: how many squares away you can stand and still read the scale (0 to 5, default 2). A wall or a shut door in the way hides it.
description=[*]Digital Scale in bathroom counters (Build 42 only): how often it turns up in bathroom counter loot, 0 to 20, default 3, 0 is never.
description=[*]Digital Scale on bathroom floors (Build 42 only): the percent chance that a big enough home or motel bathroom in an unexplored area gets one standing on its floor, default 20, 0 is never.
description=[/list]
description=

If another mod replaces the character screen, this fails safe: the scale keeps working and the Info tab simply stays as that mod or vanilla draws it.
